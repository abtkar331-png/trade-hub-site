Refund Policy

Clear rules on refunds for a digital product that unlocks instantly.

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · Effective: 30 September 2026

The short version. A Trade Hub membership unlocks the full digital library the moment it is paid for, so it is generally non-refundable once access has been granted. But if you were charged in error, charged twice, or could not access what you paid for, we will refund you. Write to us within 7 days and we will sort it out.

1. Why digital access changes things

When your payment confirms, your account is immediately granted the complete video academy, the analysis feed, the AI assistant and the strategy tools. The entire product is delivered at once — there is nothing left to ship or hold back. By completing checkout you acknowledge this immediate delivery and, where local law provides a cooling-off period for digital content, you agree that performance begins immediately and consent to losing that withdrawal right once access is granted.

2. When we do refund

We issue a full refund in these situations:

  • Duplicate payment — you were charged more than once for the same term. We refund the extra charges in full, always.
  • Payment taken but no access — your payment succeeded but your membership did not activate, and we cannot fix it for you.
  • Charged in error — you were billed for something you did not buy, or for the wrong amount.
  • Unauthorised payment — your card or wallet was used without your permission, confirmed by your bank or provider.
  • Service failure on our side — a prolonged outage or defect that we cannot remedy leaves the service substantially unusable for your paid term.
  • Overpaid crypto — you sent more cryptocurrency than the invoice required. We return the surplus.
  • We close a compliant account — if we terminate your access for our own convenience while you are in good standing, we refund the unused part of your term pro rata.

Where local consumer protection law gives you a stronger right than this policy, that law applies and we honour it.

3. When we do not refund

  • You changed your mind after the content was unlocked.
  • Trading losses. Trade Hub sells education and software, never outcomes. Losing money in the market is not a service defect and is never grounds for a refund — please re-read our risk disclaimer.
  • The content did not meet a profit expectation. We guarantee no profit, and we are explicit about that before you pay.
  • You did not use it. Not opening the app during your paid term does not create a refund right.
  • Partial terms. We do not refund unused days when you simply stop using the service. Memberships are fixed-term and do not auto-renew, so nothing further will ever be charged.
  • Breach of terms. Accounts suspended for account sharing, content redistribution, fraud or AML concerns are not refunded.
  • Third-party costs. Blockchain network fees, card issuer charges, currency conversion losses and third-party on-ramp fees are not ours and cannot be returned by us.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Write to us from the contact page within 7 days of the charge.
  2. Include your order ID, the email on the account, the date and amount, and a short description of what happened.
  3. For crypto payments, include the transaction hash (TXID).

We acknowledge every request within 2 business days and decide within 7 business days. If we need more information we will ask once, clearly.

5. How refunds are paid out

  • Card payments are refunded to the original card. Your bank typically posts it in 5–10 business days.
  • Cryptocurrency payments are refunded in the same asset to a wallet address you confirm in writing, normally within 3 business days of approval. The network fee is deducted from the refunded amount, and we are not responsible for value changes caused by exchange-rate movement between payment and refund.

We refund to the original payment method wherever possible. We will not send a refund to a different person's card or wallet.

6. Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, please contact us first. We resolve genuine problems quickly and a direct refund reaches you faster than a bank dispute.

Opening a chargeback without contacting us suspends the account while the dispute is investigated. Where a chargeback is raised in bad faith — for example after substantial use of the content — we reserve the right to contest it with our access records and to decline future service.

7. Contact

All refund requests and questions go through the contact page. A real person reads every message.

Check this policy against the consumer protection law of every market you sell into — several jurisdictions grant digital-content withdrawal rights that override a blanket no-refund rule.
