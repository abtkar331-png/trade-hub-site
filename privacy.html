1. Our approach

We collect the minimum data needed to run a subscription service, and nothing beyond it. We do not sell personal data, we do not rent it, and we do not run advertising trackers or behavioural profiling on this website.

2. What we collect

Account data

When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, display name and profile picture URL, plus a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password.

Subscription and payment data

For each purchase we store the plan, amount, currency, order reference, payment status, transaction identifier, the payment network used, and timestamps. This is the record we need to give you access, support you, and meet accounting obligations.

Usage data

Which lessons and features you open, and your progress through courses, so the app can resume where you left off and so we can see which content is useful.

Technical data

IP address, browser and device type, operating system, language preference, and server log entries. These are needed to deliver the service, keep it secure, and diagnose faults.

Communications

If you contact support we keep your messages and our replies so we can follow up and improve.

3. What we never collect

We never receive or store your card number, expiry date or CVV, your crypto private keys or seed phrase, your Google password, or your broker account credentials. Card data is entered directly on our payment processor's PCI DSS compliant pages and never reaches our servers.

We also do not ask for government identity documents for an ordinary subscription. In the rare case where a compliance review requires identity verification, that check is performed by our regulated payment partner, not by us — see the AML & Compliance Policy.

4. Why we process it — and our legal bases

  • To perform our contract with you — creating your account, taking payment, granting and maintaining access, providing support.
  • Our legitimate interests — keeping the service secure, preventing fraud and abuse, understanding aggregate product usage, and improving the platform.
  • Legal obligation — keeping transaction records for tax and accounting, and responding to lawful requests.
  • Consent — where we ask for it specifically, for example optional product emails. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who need it to make the platform work, and only for that purpose:

  • Cryptomus — payment processing. Receives the order reference and amount; processes card and crypto payment data under its own privacy policy.
  • Google — authentication (sign-in) and web fonts.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers — running the servers and database that host the service.
  • Email delivery provider — sending transactional messages such as your activation confirmation.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. If our business is ever transferred, your data may move with it under the same protections, and we will notify you.

6. Cookies and local storage

This website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. What we store on your device is strictly functional:

  • th-theme — your light/dark preference.
  • th-lang — your language preference (English or Arabic).
  • th-cookies — records that you have seen the cookie notice.
  • th-order — your current order reference, kept only for the browser session so the payment status page can find your order.

These stay in your own browser, are never transmitted to us as tracking data, and you can clear them at any time through your browser settings. Clearing them only resets your preferences.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data — while your account exists, then deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • Payment and transaction records — up to 7 years after the transaction, as tax and accounting law requires. These survive account deletion because we are legally obliged to keep them.
  • Usage data — up to 24 months, then aggregated or deleted.
  • Server logs — up to 90 days.
  • Support correspondence — up to 24 months after the ticket closes.

8. Security

We protect data with TLS encryption in transit, encryption at rest for our databases, signed and verified payment webhooks, restricted internal access on a need-to-know basis, and regular dependency and configuration review.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your personal data and poses a real risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where that happens we rely on appropriate safeguards — an adequacy decision, or standard contractual clauses with the provider — so your data keeps an equivalent level of protection.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your account and personal data (except records we must keep by law);
  • export your data in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on it;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, write to us from the contact page. We respond within 30 days and never charge for a first request.

11. Children

Trade Hub is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has created an account, tell us and we will delete it.

12. Changes and contact

If we change this policy materially we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app or by email. For any privacy question or request, reach us through the contact page.

This policy is written to be genuinely informative rather than defensive. It is not legal advice; have it reviewed by a qualified data protection lawyer for your jurisdiction before launch, and fill in your legal entity name and data controller details.
