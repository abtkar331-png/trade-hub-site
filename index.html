What Trade Hub is

A subscription platform for trading education and market tools

Trade Hub is a digital software and education service. A membership gives you access to learning content and analysis tools inside the app. It is not an investment fund, a broker, or a money-management service — you always trade through your own broker account and make your own decisions.

Your subscription is a digital product, delivered instantly and entirely online.

Included with every membership
  • Video academy — structured courses from fundamentals to advanced strategy.
  • Market analysis — educational technical analysis and trade ideas with live charts.
  • AI assistant — ask questions and understand indicators, 24/7.
  • Strategy tools — a rules-based automation helper you configure and control.
  • Community & support — learn alongside other members and reach the team directly.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly is Trade Hub?+

Trade Hub is a subscription app for trading education and market tools. You get a video academy, live market analysis, an AI assistant, strategy tools, and a community — across Android, iOS, Web, and Windows.

What am I paying for when I subscribe?+

A digital membership that unlocks all of the app's learning content and tools for a fixed period (30, 90, or 365 days). It's a software and education service — not an investment or money-management product.

How do I get access after paying?+

Once your payment is confirmed, we activate your membership on your account and everything unlocks inside the app — there is nothing separate to install.

Which payment methods can I use?+

Visa, Mastercard, or cryptocurrency such as USDT, BTC and ETH. Contact us and we will send you the payment details — we never see your card details.

Is this financial advice?+

No. Everything in Trade Hub is for education only and is not financial or investment advice. Trading carries real risk, and all decisions are your own. Please read the risk disclaimer above.

Can I cancel?+

Yes. Memberships are fixed-term and don't lock you in — simply don't renew. Your access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

Do you guarantee profits?+

No — and you shouldn't trust anyone who says they do. We sell education and tools, not outcomes. Any figures or past results shown are for educational purposes only and are not a promise of anything.

Is the service available in my country?+

The service is available in most countries, except the United States and countries subject to international sanctions. If you're unsure, contact us before paying.
