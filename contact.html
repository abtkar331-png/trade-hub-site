Before you write about a payment

Having these to hand gets your issue resolved in one reply instead of three:

  • your order ID (format th-xxxxx-xxxxx);
  • the email address on the account;
  • the date, amount and currency of the payment;
  • for crypto, the transaction hash (TXID);
  • a screenshot of any error you saw.

We will never ask you for your card's CVV, your full card number, your crypto private key or seed phrase, or your Google password. Anyone who does is not us — report it to abtkar331@gmail.com.
