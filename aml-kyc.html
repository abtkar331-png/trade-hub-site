AML & Compliance Policy

How we keep the platform clean: anti-money-laundering, sanctions and payment integrity.

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · Effective: 30 September 2026

1. Our commitment

Trade Hub sells digital educational subscriptions. We hold no client funds, operate no wallets on behalf of users, offer no exchange or money transmission service, and carry out no trading for anyone. Even so, we accept cryptocurrency and card payments, and we take seriously our responsibility not to let that channel be misused.

We are committed to preventing our platform being used for money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, or payment fraud, and we cooperate fully with lawful requests from competent authorities.

2. Regulated payment partners

We do not process payments ourselves. All payments run through Cryptomus and its processing partners, who operate their own AML/KYC programmes, transaction monitoring, blockchain analytics and sanctions screening. Card payments are additionally subject to the card schemes' and the acquiring institution's compliance controls.

This means the heaviest compliance lifting sits with parties equipped and authorised to do it, and that our own handling of sensitive identity data is kept to the minimum.

3. Restricted persons and jurisdictions

We do not knowingly provide the service to:

  • citizens or residents of the United States of America;
  • persons located in jurisdictions subject to comprehensive international sanctions;
  • individuals or entities named on applicable sanctions lists, including those maintained by the UN, EU, UK (OFSI) and US (OFAC);
  • anyone using the service to facilitate activity that is criminal under applicable law.

By subscribing you represent and warrant that none of the above applies to you, and that the funds you pay with are lawfully yours and derive from a legitimate source.

4. What we monitor

Proportionate to our low-risk business model, we watch for:

  • payment amounts that do not match the invoice, or repeated over- and under-payments;
  • an unusual number of accounts or payments linked to one card, wallet, device or IP;
  • rapid purchase-and-chargeback patterns, or refund requests to a third party's card or wallet;
  • attempts to buy access from a restricted jurisdiction, including via proxy or VPN concealment;
  • payments flagged by our processor's blockchain analytics as connected to sanctioned addresses, darknet markets or known theft.

Our payment endpoint verifies every webhook signature cryptographically and independently re-checks the amount and currency against our own order record before access is granted, so a tampered or replayed confirmation cannot unlock a membership.

5. When we may ask for verification

An ordinary subscription requires no identity documents. We may, however, request verification where:

  • a transaction is flagged by our payment partner;
  • there is reasonable suspicion of fraud or unauthorised card use;
  • a refund is requested to a payment method other than the original;
  • we are legally required to establish who a user is.

Any such check is carried out by our regulated payment partner through its own secure flow. Trade Hub does not collect or store identity documents itself.

6. What happens on a red flag

  • The transaction is held and access is not granted, or is suspended pending review.
  • We contact the account holder at their registered email, unless doing so is legally prohibited.
  • Where required, the matter is reported to the relevant authority through the appropriate channel.
  • Confirmed abuse results in permanent termination without refund, per our Terms of Service.

Funds identified as proceeds of crime are handled as the competent authority and our payment partner direct — they are not simply returned.

7. Record keeping

We retain transaction records, order references and related correspondence for at least 7 years in line with financial record-keeping obligations. These records survive account deletion, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

8. Reporting a concern

If you believe our platform is being misused, or that your payment method was used without your authorisation, tell us immediately through the contact page. Reports are treated confidentially and reviewed promptly.

This policy reflects proportionate controls for a digital education merchant that holds no client funds. If your jurisdiction brings your activity within a regulated category, obtain specialist compliance advice and appoint a responsible officer before launch.
